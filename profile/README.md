
# Wakka – Urban Bus Transport Management Platform

**Wakka** is a smart transport management system designed to streamline and modernize intercity and urban bus operations in emerging markets. By combining digital ticketing, real-time GPS tracking, and intelligent dashboards, Wakka empowers passengers, drivers, and bus operators with a seamless and transparent transport experience.

---

## 🌍 What is Wakka?

Wakka is a mobile-first platform that enables:

- 📲 Easy ticket booking from any device
- 🎫 Secure QR code tickets for smooth boarding
- 🚌 Real-time tracking of buses between cities
- 📡 GPS-powered location sharing for safety and transparency
- 📊 Dashboards for operators to manage routes, revenue, and performance
- 💬 Notifications and alerts for passengers and drivers

---

## 👥 Who is it for?

- **Passengers** – Search, book, and track bus trips effortlessly.
- **Drivers** – Receive schedules and validate tickets easily.
- **Bus Operators** – Digitize operations, manage fleets, and view analytics.
- **Transport Unions & Cooperatives** – Coordinate multiple operators and monitor performance.

---

## 💡 Why Wakka?

Millions rely on buses as their primary form of intercity transportation. Yet, most operators lack the digital infrastructure to optimize services, reduce fraud, and deliver a reliable passenger experience.

Wakka solves this with:

- Transparent ticketing
- Real-time bus tracking
- Operator insights and analytics
- Accessible tools for small and mid-size operators

---

## 🌐 Availability

Wakka is designed to work across web and mobile platforms. It can be deployed in regions with limited infrastructure and supports local payment options and SMS notifications.
